PBW01

Canonical embedding

L ↪ Uℤ(L)

Every Lie ring embeds in its universal enveloping algebra over ℤ—without choosing a basis.

  • UR(A) ≃ SymR(A) when A is Abelian
  • Ordered PBW monomials span for an ordered basis
  • A triangular representation gives the full PBW statement

Universal enveloping02

Quotients commute with U

UR(L/I) ≃ UR(L)/⟨I⟩

The enveloping algebra of a Lie quotient is the expected associative quotient. This powers the functorial reductions used throughout.

Exact low degrees03

The filtrations first agree

δ2(L) = γ2(L)δ3(L) = γ3(L)

The degree-two equality holds over every commutative base ring; degree three holds for every Lie ring.

Free Lie rings04

Agreement in every degree

δn(F) = γn(F)

For a free Lie ring F and every positive degree n, the dimension and lower-central filtrations coincide.

Degree four05

A sharp factor-two bound

2δ4(L) ⊆ γ4(L)

Bartholdi–Passi’s factor-two theorem, formalized for every Lie ring.

Dimension centrality06

Brackets recover the classical series

[δn(L), L] = γn+1(L) [δm(L), δn(L)] ⊆ γm+n(L)

More generally, augmentation-ideal action realizes the full relative lower-central tail: A·ω(L)n = [A,nL].

Associated graded07

The comparison is a graded Lie map

grγ(L) → grδ(L)

Both filtrations define associated graded Lie algebras. The canonical image is homogeneous, and its graded cokernel is Abelian.
