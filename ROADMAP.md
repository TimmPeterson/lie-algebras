# PBW roadmap

The first PBW target is the global ordered-basis theorem for a Lie algebra whose underlying module
is free. The public statement is already fixed as `PBW.FreeModulePBW`.

The basis-free Abelian case is already complete in `PBW.Abelian`: it constructs the canonical
equivalence `U_R(L) ≃ₐ[R] SymmetricAlgebra R L` over any commutative ring.

The planned proof follows Cartan–Eilenberg, *Homological Algebra*, Chapter XIII, §3. This route is
preferable for the first implementation because it works directly with an ordered basis and proves
exactly that the ordered products form a basis of `U(L)`.

1. **Complete:** prove the rearrangement lemma and surjectivity of `PBW.orderedPBWMap`.
2. **Complete:** construct the degree-recursive candidate action of `L` on
   `MvPolynomial ι R` used in Cartan–Eilenberg Lemma 3.5.
3. Verify `CartanActionLieCompatible`; Jacobi is the critical confluence calculation.
4. **Complete:** evaluate ordered products at `1` and prove that a compatible action gives linear
   independence.
5. **Complete conditional on step 3:** package the resulting bijection as a linear equivalence and
   derive injectivity of `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.ι` for free underlying modules.

The file `PBW.Reduction` also proves that, for a free Lie algebra, injectivity of the canonical map
into its enveloping algebra is equivalent to the Magnus–Witt embedding into the free associative
algebra. This prevents mathlib's existing equivalence
`U(FreeLie(X)) ≃ FreeAlgebra(X)` from being accidentally treated as an embedding theorem: that
equivalence follows from universal properties, while Magnus–Witt injectivity is the missing fact.

Independently of the still-open general ordered-basis statement, the following exact integral
target is now complete (with no basis, flatness, torsion-freeness, or representation parameter):

```lean
theorem canonicalMap_injective_int
    (L : Type*) [LieRing L] :
    Function.Injective
      (UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.ι ℤ : L → UEA ℤ L)
```

There are two genuinely different extension steps. Govorov--Lazard transport from finite free
modules proves PBW for flat modules; over `ℤ` this includes torsion-free Abelian groups.
It does **not** include arbitrary Lie rings with torsion. For the unconditional theorem above, the
supplied Higgins proof instead shows that its Baer obstruction vanishes for direct sums of cyclic
modules and that this obstruction commutes with directed limits. Since finitely generated
`ℤ`-modules are direct sums of cyclic modules and every `ℤ`-module is their directed limit, this
gives the PID corollary for every underlying Abelian group.

Mathlib supplies useful pieces of the direct-limit and tensor-product infrastructure, and it proves
that torsion-free modules over Dedekind domains are flat; `PBW.Conditions` exposes the latter as
convenient named theorems. It does not supply the Govorov--Lazard theorem, Higgins's Baer
obstruction, or its directed-limit theorem. Keeping these layers separate is essential: the
Cartan--Eilenberg proof assumes a basis, and the flat-module extension still does not cover Lie
rings with torsion.

The Higgins implementation is spread across the `PBW.Higgins*` files. The following parts are
complete without
any unproved assumptions:

1. the canonical free presentation `ker(P → M) → P → M`;
2. the ideals `K(P)`, `Q̃`, and `Z`, and `B(M) = (K(P) ∩ Q̃)/Z`;
3. exact tensor and symmetric presentation rows, including `Q̃ = ker(T(P) → T(M))`;
4. the equality of `K(M)` with the two-sided ideal generated by the elementary degree-one
   commutators;
5. the product containments `K(P)Q̃ + Q̃K(P) ⊆ Z` and descent of the commuting left and right
   `T(M)`-actions to `C(M)`;
6. surjectivity of `C(M)=K(P)/Z → K(M)` and the proof that `B(M)=0` makes it an equivalence;
7. vanishing of `B(M)` for projective integral modules (hence for free Abelian groups), by a
   split-presentation tensor retraction;
8. Higgins's Lie structures on `C(M)` and `K(M)`, including (L1)--(L3), and proof that the
   comparison `C(M) → K(M)` preserves both tensor actions and the bracket;
9. lift-independence of the bracket and uniqueness of every Lie-structure morphism out of `C(M)`.
10. vanishing of `B(M)` for arbitrary internal direct sums of cyclic Abelian groups, including
    finite cyclic summands; this is
    `PBW.Higgins.obstructionVanishes_of_directSumOfCyclic` in `PBW.HigginsCyclic`.
11. finite-stage compatibility for the directed union of finitely generated subgroups, followed
    by unconditional vanishing `PBW.Higgins.obstructionVanishes_all M` for every Abelian group.
    This is complete in `PBW.HigginsDirectLimit`.
12. transport across `C(M) ≃ K(M)`, proving that the concrete commutator structure `K(M)` has
    Higgins's universal property for every Abelian group. This is complete in `PBW.HigginsPBW`.
13. Higgins's filtration `J(n)` of the enveloping-algebra defining ideal: the implementation proves
    `J(0)=J(1)=0`, monotonicity, `J(n) ⊆ J ∩ Tₙ`, stability under left and right multiplication by
    degree-one generators, and `⋃ₙ J(n)=J`. The graded pieces `J(n)/J(n-1)` and their degree-shifting
    left and right generator maps are also constructed in `PBW.HigginsFiltration`. These maps are
    assembled into commuting left and right tensor-algebra actions on `⨁ₙ J(n)/J(n-1)`. The
    relation class is defined as a bilinear bracket and all three Higgins identities (L1)--(L3)
    are proved; `PBW.Higgins.higginsFiltrationLieStructure` packages the result.
14. `PBW.HigginsEmbedding` constructs the leading homogeneous-component comparison, proves its
    injectivity from Higgins universality, and descends `J(n)` to `J(1)=0` for degree-one tensors.
    It then compares the explicit tensor quotient with mathlib's enveloping algebra and exports
    `PBW.canonicalMap_injective_int`.

The local file named `emanuela petracci.pdf` is only a one-page browser/Wayback cover rather than
the thesis text, so no formal step currently depends on it.

The supplied references are kept unchanged in `lit/`.

## Bartholdi--Passi low-degree layer

The following low-degree results are complete:

1. `UEA.quotientEquivLieIdeal`: `U(L/I) ≃ U(L)/⟨I⟩`, for every Lie ideal over every
   commutative base ring.
2. `dimensionSubring_two_eq_lowerCentralSeries_one`: `δ₂(L) = γ₂(L)`, again over every
   commutative base ring. Its proof uses the square-zero extension of the abelianization.
3. `dimensionSubring_three_eq_lowerCentralSeries_two`: `δ₃(L) = γ₃(L)` for every Lie
   ring. Its proof uses Higgins universality to polarize the bracket integrally and a three-step
   triangular representation detecting `γ₂/γ₃`.

Bartholdi--Passi Theorem 4.1 is now complete as
`FreeLieDimension.dimensionSubring_succ_eq_lowerCentralSeries`: for every free Lie ring over
`ℤ`, `δₙ(F)=γₙ(F)` in all degrees. Its proof uses the integral canonical embedding from
`PBW.HigginsEmbedding` and compares the bracketed-word and associative-word length filtrations.

The remaining printed proof of `2δ₄ ⊆ γ₄` for arbitrary Lie rings uses a preabelian
presentation; that presentation/Smith-normal-form layer remains future work.
