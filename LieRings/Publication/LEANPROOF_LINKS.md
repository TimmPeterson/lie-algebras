`LieRings/Publication/Results.lean` on the current `main` branch. Its existing
lines must never be edited or moved.


## Scope of this catalogue

Only complete statements matching the manuscript are listed. The manuscript's
full PBW theorem, PBW-collection corollary, general Passi--Sicking theorem,
finite-generation reduction proposition, additive-extension theorem, and its
intermediate technical lemmas are not currently exported in precisely their
published formulations, so no publication links are claimed for them here.
